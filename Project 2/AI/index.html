Our Story

A Little Corner of Comfort in Lagos

Brew Haven Café was born from a simple dream: to bring world-class coffee culture to Lagos without losing the warmth and soul of Nigerian hospitality. Founded in 2019 by childhood friends Adaeze Nwosu and Tunde Bakare, our café blends the bold flavors of locally-sourced beans from the Jos Plateau with beloved Nigerian comfort food.

Every cup we pour and every plate we serve carries the spirit of community. Whether you're catching up with friends over a pot of chai, powering through emails with a flat white, or digging into a plate of jollof rice at lunch — you're family here.

  • ☉ Locally-sourced beans from the Jos Plateau
  • 🍼 Nigerian-inspired menu made fresh daily
  • 🌍 Eco-friendly packaging and composting
